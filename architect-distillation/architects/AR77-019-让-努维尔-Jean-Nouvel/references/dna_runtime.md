# AR77-019｜DNA Runtime
Select only 2–4 DNA. Every selected DNA must answer a named conflict and leave a verifiable spatial trace.

## 01｜SPECIFICITY_BEFORE_TYPE
**Judgement:** project-specific conditions outrank generic typology.
**Trigger:** brief could produce the same building anywhere.
**Spatial Operation:** reorganize orientation, access, adjacency, view or section from non-interchangeable conditions.
**Boundary:** specificity must be factual, not invented narrative.
**Failure:** decorative “local identity”.
**Trace:** at least one plan/section decision changes because of a verified condition.

## 02｜CONSTRAINT_TO_CONCEPT
**Judgement:** constraints can generate the concept.
**Trigger:** strong limits appear to reduce design freedom.
**Spatial Operation:** convert one verified constraint into a relational organizing rule.
**Boundary:** code/safety constraints remain obligations.
**Failure:** romanticizing dysfunction.
**Trace:** the constraint and resulting spatial rule can be diagrammed causally.

## 03｜REVEAL_CONCEAL
**Judgement:** perception can be sequenced rather than fully exposed.
**Trigger:** privacy, anticipation, exhibition, civic presence or approach matters.
**Spatial Operation:** calibrate thresholds, sightlines, opacity, distance and timing.
**Boundary:** never assume glass or dark enclosure.
**Failure:** theatrical concealment that harms wayfinding/safety.
**Trace:** path diagram shows changing visibility.

## 04｜LAYERED_PERCEPTION
**Judgement:** multiple spatial readings may coexist through depth, reflection, overlap or framing.
**Trigger:** site/building/interior relations need simultaneous reading.
**Spatial Operation:** align layers, foreground/background, views and boundaries.
**Boundary:** must survive without a specific material palette.
**Failure:** visual noise.
**Trace:** key viewpoints demonstrate distinct readable layers.

## 05｜LIGHT_AS_RELATION
**Judgement:** light is an organizer of cultural, environmental and perceptual relations.
**Trigger:** orientation, display, climate, atmosphere or time variation is decisive.
**Spatial Operation:** control admission, reflection, shade, contrast and temporal change.
**Boundary:** lighting effect must respect heat/glare/conservation/safety.
**Failure:** iconic light effect detached from use.
**Trace:** section/daylight study links light condition to program and movement.

## 06｜SITE_INCLUSION
**Judgement:** an external condition can become part of internal organization.
**Trigger:** lake, garden, street, horizon, heritage or public realm is dominant.
**Spatial Operation:** extend/receive it through ground, path, view, threshold or program relation.
**Boundary:** inclusion is relational, not literal copying.
**Failure:** scenic backdrop only.
**Trace:** removing the site condition would invalidate the spatial organization.

## 07｜PRODUCTIVE_CONTRADICTION
**Judgement:** apparently opposed qualities can coexist when they sharpen the project’s meaning/use.
**Trigger:** open/protected, present/absent, monumental/intimate, real/virtual tensions are genuine.
**Spatial Operation:** assign each side a spatial role and controlled interface.
**Boundary:** contradiction is not arbitrary complexity.
**Failure:** spectacle without operational benefit.
**Trace:** comparison shows why both conditions are necessary.

## 08｜TECHNOLOGY_AS_INSTRUMENT
**Judgement:** technology serves a concept/performance relation rather than acting as a signature.
**Trigger:** façade, acoustic, structural, environmental or transformable systems are needed.
**Spatial Operation:** make the system perform a named spatial/environmental task.
**Boundary:** simplest viable system wins when performance is equivalent.
**Failure:** gadgetry, maintenance burden, unverified mechanism.
**Trace:** performance metric + failure/maintenance scenario + spatial consequence.
