# AR77-013｜BOUNDARIES + CONTEMPORARY CORRECTIONS

## Climate
Complex envelopes require solar, thermal, moisture, wind, rain, snow/ice (where relevant), drainage, condensation and local hazard verification. Sculptural geometry is not environmental performance.

## Accessibility
Discovery and varied circulation must preserve dignified step-free routes, clear destinations, resting, gradients, headroom and equivalent experience.

## Fire
Atrium, interconnection, complex circulation and differentiated parts require compartmentation, smoke control, occupant load, egress and fire-service review.

## Structure
Irregular or non-orthogonal geometry demands early stability, load-path, movement, deflection, robustness and connection logic. No signature cantilever/lean is assumed.

## MEP
Coordinate plant, risers, distribution, access, replacement and controls before geometry freezes. Complex ceilings/envelopes can hide severe conflicts.

## Acoustics
Where sound matters, specialist criteria must be causal from the beginning. Visual form cannot substitute for acoustic simulation/testing.

## Privacy / Security
Social collision is not universally desirable. Preserve concentration, confidential work, safeguarding and controlled access where required.

## Operations
Test queueing, events, loading, after-hours zoning, cleaning, staffing, public/private transitions and independent operation.

## Maintenance
Curved/angled envelopes, inaccessible surfaces and custom components require drainage, cleaning, inspection, replacement and safe access strategies.

## Cost
Iterative formal ambition must be tied to value. Compare simple and complex means that achieve the same program/performance effect.

## Procurement
Confirm fabrication capability, tolerances, sequencing, digital handoff, mockups, supply chain and replacement before relying on custom geometry.

## Lifecycle
Test embodied/operational carbon, durability, adaptability, disassembly and long-term availability of custom components.

## Digital Coordination
A shared 3D model can coordinate complexity but is never proof of code, performance, fabrication quality or architectural value.

## Historical Correction Rule
When a documented Gehry mechanism conflicts with contemporary requirements, preserve the underlying judgement only if useful; redesign the means. Historical precedent is never a waiver.
