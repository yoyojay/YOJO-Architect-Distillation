# AR77-013｜DESIGN DNA RUNTIME

> DNA = distilled decision mechanism, never a motif list.

## D1｜RELATIONSHIP BEFORE OBJECT
**Judgement Meaning:** user/program relationships should generate primary spatial order before exterior image.
**Trigger:** performance, collaboration, community or interaction is central.
**Spatial Operation:** map who needs to see, hear, meet, separate or cross; organize adjacencies and shared anchors accordingly.
**Boundary:** privacy, security, acoustics, crowding, independent operation.
**Failure Mode:** spectacular container with weak program relations.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** relationship diagram and plan show direct consequences.

## D2｜MODEL AS ARGUMENT
**Judgement Meaning:** a model is a tool for discovering and disproving architecture, not only showing it.
**Trigger:** plan/elevation cannot resolve volume, scale, site relation, movement or interacting parts.
**Spatial Operation:** create quick alternate physical/digital models at useful scales; record what each test changes.
**Boundary:** model fidelity must suit the question; digital detail is not evidence by itself.
**Failure Mode:** endless sculptural iterations without criteria.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** model sequence documents question → alternative → decision.

## D3｜DIFFERENTIATED PARTS
**Judgement Meaning:** complex programs may benefit from legible, non-uniform parts composed into a whole.
**Trigger:** distinct groups/functions/context edges require different spatial identities.
**Spatial Operation:** articulate masses/rooms/structures by role, then compose adjacencies, overlaps, gaps and connectors.
**Boundary:** wayfinding, envelope continuity, structure, services, cost.
**Failure Mode:** fragmentation for appearance.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** each differentiated part maps to a real program/context role.

## D4｜MOVEMENT + ENCOUNTER
**Judgement Meaning:** circulation can create encounter, discovery and changing perception.
**Trigger:** public/cultural/institutional programs benefit from interaction and sequence.
**Spatial Operation:** shape approach, connectors, stairs/ramps/streets/atria/landings around verified encounters and destinations.
**Boundary:** accessibility, egress, crowd control, fatigue, clarity.
**Failure Mode:** spectacle circulation or accidental confusion.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** path storyboard shows purpose, choice and destination.

## D5｜EMOTIONAL COMPOSITION
**Judgement Meaning:** scale, proximity, compression/release, light and juxtaposition can carry feeling without becoming image-first design.
**Trigger:** cultural/civic/collective experience requires emotional intensity.
**Spatial Operation:** tune relative scale, spatial contrast, enclosure and light after program relations are sound.
**Boundary:** comfort, dignity, acoustics, code, operations.
**Failure Mode:** dramatic composition that compromises use.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** section/model links each experiential move to a program moment.

## D6｜MATERIAL DIRECTNESS
**Judgement Meaning:** available material/craft can be used honestly and inventively rather than as prestige finish.
**Trigger:** local supply, fabrication limits, budget or construction culture is known.
**Spatial Operation:** test actual material behavior, jointing, tolerance and craft; turn constraints into architectural order.
**Boundary:** weathering, fire, durability, toxicity, maintenance, procurement.
**Failure Mode:** chain-link/corrugated/titanium cosplay.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** sample/mockup or construction logic explains the choice.

## D7｜TECHNICAL TRANSLATION
**Judgement Meaning:** complex architectural intent must be translated into buildable, coordinated information.
**Trigger:** geometry/performance exceeds ordinary 2D coordination.
**Spatial Operation:** establish shared 3D model, specialist constraints, tolerances, interfaces and verification checkpoints.
**Boundary:** software is not design authority; responsibility and field tolerances remain explicit.
**Failure Mode:** complexity generated because software can describe it.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** coordinated model resolves structure/envelope/MEP/fabrication conflicts.

## D8｜CONTEXTUAL DIALOGUE
**Judgement Meaning:** a project can be distinct without ignoring the physical/cultural city around it.
**Trigger:** strong urban, historic, infrastructural or landscape context.
**Spatial Operation:** identify scales, routes, edges, alignments, contrasts and public connections; decide deliberately which to continue or challenge.
**Boundary:** heritage, planning, neighbor impacts, public realm.
**Failure Mode:** isolated icon justified by vague “contrast.”
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** context diagram shows specific reciprocities or deliberate differences.

## Selection Lock

Select 3–5 only. Every selected DNA must materially affect a drawing, model or test. Unselected DNA cannot re-enter Recommendation, Prompt or Handoff.
