# AR77-008 Renzo Piano｜Runtime Design DNA

Source basis: original 008 `design-dna.md`.

Select **4–7** DNA.

---

## D01｜SERVICE_BEFORE_SIGNATURE｜服务先于签名

**Problem**  
The project is being covered by a personal visual signature.

**Principle**  
Architecture first serves a concrete social task and future responsibility.

**Operation**  
Lock users, operators, future change, exclusion mechanisms, and accessibility before spatial language.

**Evidence required**  
Conflict matrix + priority record.

**Risk**  
“Service” becomes trying to satisfy everyone.

**Correction**  
Translate opinions into explicit conflicts and trade-offs.

---

## D02｜BUILDING_AS_RESEARCH｜建造即研究

**Problem**  
Concept is fixed too early and engineering merely implements the image.

**Principle**  
Materials, fabrication, structure, prototypes, and site feedback can correct design.

**Operation**  
Problem → model → mockup → test → revise → freeze.

**Evidence required**  
Prototype record + decision log.

**Risk**  
Research expands without limit.

**Correction**  
Every test has pass criteria, budget limit, and decision deadline.

---

## D03｜PIECE_BY_PIECE｜由构件组织整体

**Problem**  
Overall form is disconnected from fabrication, assembly, and maintenance.

**Principle**  
A critical component can carry structural, environmental, manufacturing, and replacement logic.

**Operation**  
Define piece, interface, repetition, variation, tolerance, replacement, and spare strategy.

**Evidence required**  
Piece matrix + assembly / replacement diagram.

**Risk**  
Too many custom parts create cost and supply-chain lock-in.

**Correction**  
Compare standard / semi-custom / custom options.

---

## D04｜COLLABORATIVE_AUTHORSHIP｜协作式作者关系

**Problem**  
Complex outcomes are attributed to one architect's intuition.

**Principle**  
Architecture emerges through architecture, engineering, consulting, fabrication, operation, and user collaboration.

**Operation**  
Build role–problem–decision-right matrix.

**Evidence required**  
Responsibility matrix.

**Risk**  
Responsibility becomes blurred.

**Correction**  
Record final decision owner, interface responsibility, dispute, and change.

---

## D05｜TASK_CALIBRATED_LIGHT｜任务校准的光

**Problem**  
Glass, skylights, or brightness are assumed to mean good light.

**Principle**  
Light must be calibrated to display, conservation, orientation, health, thermal load, and time.

**Operation**  
Build illuminance / uniformity / glare / UV / heat / shading / maintenance matrix.

**Evidence required**  
Light-performance diagram.

**Risk**  
Complex daylight systems become hard to clean and replace.

**Correction**  
Include maintenance access, shading redundancy, and artificial-light fallback.

---

## D06｜INTEGRATED_SECTION｜系统整合剖面

**Problem**  
Architecture, structure, MEP, circulation, acoustics, and smoke control are optimized separately.

**Principle**  
Section is the shared working surface of multiple systems.

**Operation**  
Overlay people, accessibility, structure, air, light, sound, equipment, maintenance, and smoke control in one section.

**Evidence required**  
Integrated section.

**Risk**  
High coupling makes changes propagate through every system.

**Correction**  
Design isolation zones, redundancy, replacement paths, and staged freeze points.

---

## D07｜PUBLIC_GROUND｜公共地面

**Problem**  
Public buildings have lobbies but no real urban public ground.

**Principle**  
Publicness depends on space, time, cost, climate, security, and operations.

**Operation**  
Map city path, free zone, opening hours, boundaries, shade, seating, night use, and event changes.

**Evidence required**  
Publicness schedule + ground plan.

**Risk**  
Public space is neutralized by commercialization, security, or bad weather.

**Correction**  
Define free / paid boundaries and extreme-weather strategy.

---

## D08｜TECH_VISIBILITY_BY_NEED｜技术按需显隐

**Problem**  
“High-tech” is interpreted as exposing all structure and services.

**Principle**  
Technical visibility depends on whether it improves understanding, experience, and maintenance.

**Operation**  
For each system ask why visible, who benefits, how maintained, whether hidden is better.

**Evidence required**  
Experience readability + maintenance accessibility diagrams.

**Risk**  
Exposure creates visual noise; concealment reduces maintainability.

**Correction**  
Separate experiential readability from maintenance access.

---

## D09｜PLACE_WITHOUT_PASTICHE｜场所回应而非仿古

**Problem**  
Local identity is reduced to traditional shapes or material appliqué.

**Principle**  
Place enters through scale, terrain, climate, path, craft, supply, and social relationships.

**Operation**  
Build place-fact table and separate transferable mechanisms from non-transferable symbols.

**Evidence required**  
Place / stakeholder map.

**Risk**  
Cross-cultural projects become exoticized.

**Correction**  
Clarify cultural decision rights, benefit, IP, and long-term operational role.

---

## D10｜CLIMATE_AS_EXPERIENCE｜气候成为体验

**Problem**  
Environmental systems remain hidden in reports or green labels.

**Principle**  
Wind, light, temperature, and water can be both performance and bodily experience.

**Operation**  
Define seasons, modes, perceptible nodes, active / passive boundaries, and control logic.

**Evidence required**  
Climate-mode diagram.

**Risk**  
Natural ventilation can fail in pollution, noise, humidity, or extreme heat.

**Correction**  
Provide hybrid mode, closed mode, dehumidification, and air-quality monitoring.

---

## D11｜PROTOTYPE_LADDER｜原型阶梯

**Problem**  
Complex decisions rely only on rendering or a single digital model.

**Principle**  
Different scales of models answer different questions; 1:1 mockups test body, material, interfaces, and construction.

**Operation**  
1:1000 → 1:200 → 1:50 → 1:5 → 1:1.

**Evidence required**  
Prototype ladder with pass / fail criteria.

**Risk**  
Prototype becomes presentation theater.

**Correction**  
Every test must leave a comparison, modification, and freeze record.

---

## D12｜LIFECYCLE_LIGHTNESS｜全生命周期轻盈

**Problem**  
“Lightness” is judged only by thin edges, transparency, or delicate structure.

**Principle**  
Lightness also involves material efficiency, energy, disassembly, adaptation, maintenance, and experience.

**Operation**  
Compare structure, embodied carbon, operating energy, lifespan, repair, replacement, and adaptability.

**Evidence required**  
Lifecycle matrix.

**Risk**  
Visual lightness hides complexity, carbon, or maintenance burden.

**Correction**  
Do not use “lightweight / sustainable” as a positive conclusion without lifecycle evidence.

---

# Combination discipline

- Select 4–7 DNA.
- D01 and D04 are mandatory.
- Complex public buildings require D06.
- Custom components require D03 and D11.
- Daylight discussion requires D05.
- Climate discussion requires D10.
- “Lightness / sustainability” requires D12.
- Cross-cultural projects require D09 and cultural-decision-right Gate.
- Every DNA must bind to a drawing, metric, model, or prototype.
