# AR77-008｜Period / Task Routes Runtime

These are AR77 runtime routes derived from the 008 source package. They are not Renzo Piano's own official period labels.

---

## R01｜PUBLIC_MACHINE｜公共机器

### Historical evidence family
Centre Pompidou period.

### Contemporary task
- urban cultural center;
- open public platform;
- flexible exhibition / civic use.

### Core judgement
Public ground, changing use, and service systems must be coordinated rather than turned into an exposed-technology image.

### Mandatory Gates
- public ground;
- accessibility;
- fire;
- acoustics;
- equipment;
- maintenance.

### Do not copy
- coloured pipes;
- external escalator;
- gerberette image.

---

## R02｜CALIBRATED_LIGHT_CRAFT｜调光构件

### Historical evidence family
Menil / travelling pavilion period.

### Contemporary task
- museum;
- archive;
- research;
- healing / learning space.

### Core judgement
Daylight must be calibrated to a specific task and expressed through a buildable, maintainable system.

### Mandatory Gates
- illuminance / task;
- glare;
- UV;
- thermal load;
- cleaning;
- replacement.

### Do not copy
- roof leaves;
- thin white roofs.

---

## R03｜INTEGRATED_MEGA_SECTION｜大型整合剖面

### Historical evidence family
Kansai Airport period.

### Contemporary task
- transport hub;
- long-span public building;
- convention / sports facility.

### Core judgement
Structure, flow, air, light, fire, orientation, expansion, and failure modes must be resolved through integrated section.

### Mandatory Gates
- crowd movement;
- structure;
- airflow;
- fire;
- wayfinding;
- expansion;
- failure mode.

### Do not copy
- wave roof;
- glider metaphor.

---

## R04｜CULTURE_CLIMATE_TRANSLATION｜文化—气候转译

### Historical evidence family
Tjibaou period.

### Contemporary task
- local culture;
- education;
- community;
- hot-humid public architecture.

### Core judgement
Translate climate, scale, path, craft, and cultural decision rights rather than consume symbolic form.

### Mandatory Gates
- cultural decision rights;
- material supply;
- ventilation modes;
- maintenance;
- operations.

### Do not copy
- shell huts;
- timber-rib exotic imagery.

---

## R05｜ECO_CIVIC_REPAIR｜生态公共修复

### Historical evidence family
California Academy / reuse / civic repair.

### Contemporary task
- park cultural facilities;
- adaptive reuse;
- campus;
- urban repair.

### Core judgement
Evaluate intervention through retained value, lifecycle carbon, operational energy, resilience, and public continuity.

### Mandatory Gates
- lifecycle carbon;
- retention rate;
- operating energy;
- resilience;
- post-occupancy evaluation.

### Do not copy
- undulating green roof;
- eco-domes;
- certification icons.

---

# Route Gate

1. Select Route from project conflict, never precedent imagery.
2. One primary Route only.
3. One secondary Route only when a distinct secondary conflict requires it.
4. R01 / R03 freedom must pass fire, acoustics, maintenance, and phased-operation correction.
5. R02 light must be task-based, not “soft natural light”.
6. R04 requires real cultural actors and decision-right evidence.
7. R05 separates design intent, certification, and actual operating performance.
