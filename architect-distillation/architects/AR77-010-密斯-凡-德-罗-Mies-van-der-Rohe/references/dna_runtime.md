# AR77-010｜Design DNA Runtime

## Selection
10 mechanisms exist. Select only 3–5. Unselected DNA cannot re-enter.

## D01｜ESSENTIAL REDUCTION
**Judgement meaning:** reduce the brief to essential architectural relations before form.
**Trigger:** brief is overloaded or image-driven.
**Spatial operation:** identify stable / changeable / public / private / structural / service relations.
**Boundary:** reduction must not erase real social or operational needs.
**Failure mode:** minimal-looking plan that actually ignores requirements.
**Verifiable trace:** one clear causal diagram can explain the main spatial order.

## D02｜STRUCTURAL-SPATIAL ORDER
**Judgement meaning:** structure should establish or release space.
**Trigger:** span, repetition, large room, open plan or system building.
**Spatial operation:** align structural logic with spatial field, section and circulation.
**Boundary:** actual structural type must be engineer-verified.
**Failure mode:** exposed structure used only as image.
**Verifiable trace:** plan and section show why support placement and span matter spatially.

## D03｜LONG-LIFE / SHORT-LIFE SEPARATION
**Judgement meaning:** distinguish durable framework from changing occupation.
**Trigger:** program churn, education, workplace, exhibition, long lifecycle.
**Spatial operation:** keep major structure/services/circulation stable while partitions/furniture/program can change.
**Boundary:** not all uses should be flexible.
**Failure mode:** flexibility pushes essential services or storage into ad hoc zones.
**Verifiable trace:** at least two plausible future layouts work without altering primary order.

## D04｜OPEN FIELD WITH ANCHORS
**Judgement meaning:** openness needs legible anchors.
**Trigger:** large shared floorplate.
**Spatial operation:** use service cores, fixed rooms, circulation or program anchors to orient changeable zones.
**Boundary:** anchors may need acoustic/fire enclosure.
**Failure mode:** empty undifferentiated hall.
**Verifiable trace:** users can identify destinations and zones without permanent room-by-room enclosure.

## D05｜RELATIONAL BOUNDARY
**Judgement meaning:** boundaries regulate degrees of relation.
**Trigger:** continuity and separation must coexist.
**Spatial operation:** tune boundary by movement, view, privacy, acoustics, climate and operation.
**Boundary:** material and geometry remain project-specific.
**Failure mode:** “transparent” is substituted for relational performance.
**Verifiable trace:** each boundary has an explicit performance role.

## D06｜DIRECTED CONTINUITY
**Judgement meaning:** continuity should be spatially legible, not vague.
**Trigger:** flowing plan, gallery, domestic or cultural sequence.
**Spatial operation:** align openings, planes, thresholds and movement to create connected but differentiated zones.
**Boundary:** egress and accessibility can override preferred sequence.
**Failure mode:** circulation cuts through incompatible uses.
**Verifiable trace:** plan shows continuous movement plus identifiable spatial territories.

## D07｜LANDSCAPE / EXTERIOR COUPLING
**Judgement meaning:** architecture can intensify the relation between occupation and exterior conditions.
**Trigger:** important landscape, court, horizon, garden, street or view.
**Spatial operation:** calibrate position, boundary, threshold and occupation relative to exterior.
**Boundary:** does not imply glass; climate/privacy/habitat are mandatory checks.
**Failure mode:** scenic view prioritized over comfort and use.
**Verifiable trace:** section and plan show why the occupied zone relates to a specific exterior condition.

## D08｜DETAIL-TO-WHOLE CONSISTENCY
**Judgement meaning:** critical details should confirm the global order.
**Trigger:** envelope, junction, tolerance or assembly decisions threaten conceptual clarity.
**Spatial operation:** simplify competing junction logics; coordinate module, support, envelope and finish.
**Boundary:** precision must be realistic for procurement and maintenance.
**Failure mode:** expensive detailing with no architectural consequence.
**Verifiable trace:** a critical detail can be explained directly from the larger structural/spatial logic.

## D09｜URBAN / CAMPUS ORDERING
**Judgement meaning:** building relations can clarify a larger urban field.
**Trigger:** campus, ensemble, repeated blocks, strong city grid.
**Spatial operation:** align / offset / frame existing axes and establish legible intervals between buildings.
**Boundary:** existing community, heritage and ecology can require deliberate breaks.
**Failure mode:** abstract grid erases context.
**Verifiable trace:** site plan explains building placement and public movement as one system.

## D10｜GROUND AND APPROACH
**Judgement meaning:** arrival and ground condition are part of architectural order.
**Trigger:** public/institutional entry, landscape threshold, urban setback.
**Spatial operation:** organize approach, threshold and occupied ground to make entry and building relation legible.
**Boundary:** does not imply stairs, podium or plaza; accessibility is mandatory.
**Failure mode:** monumental approach harms everyday access.
**Verifiable trace:** site/ground plan and section show an understandable, accessible sequence.
