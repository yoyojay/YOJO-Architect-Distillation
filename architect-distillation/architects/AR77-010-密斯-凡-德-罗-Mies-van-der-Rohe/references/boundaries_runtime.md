# AR77-010｜Boundaries + Contemporary Corrections Runtime

## Climate
Do not inherit historic glazing ratios or transparency. Test orientation, solar gain, glare, U-value, condensation, thermal bridges, shading, natural ventilation feasibility and local climate.

## Accessibility
Any ground / approach / level change must have an equivalent dignified accessible route. Universal-space rhetoric does not justify inaccessible thresholds.

## Fire
Open fields may require compartmentation, smoke control, egress separation and rated construction. Treat these as design-generating constraints.

## Structure
Clear spans, slenderness, vibration, robustness, progressive collapse and lateral systems require engineer verification. Do not infer a steel system.

## MEP
Universal or open spaces still need distribution, return air, power, data, drainage, maintenance access and plant. Integrate without pretending services disappear.

## Acoustics
Open fields can create speech/privacy/reverberation conflicts. Zone acoustically and test before preserving openness.

## Privacy
Visual connection must be separated from actual privacy requirements.

## Operations / Security
Test day/night zoning, independent access, staff oversight, cleaning, changeover and emergency use.

## Maintenance
Highly precise junctions and large transparent areas may impose cleaning, sealant, coating and replacement burdens. Detail-to-whole consistency must include maintainability.

## Cost / Procurement
Tight tolerances and custom systems may be incompatible with budget or local supply. Translate precision into a hierarchy of critical tolerances rather than universal custom detailing.

## Lifecycle
Prefer a durable primary order that can accept future change. Evaluate disassembly, replacement cycles and embodied/operational carbon.

## Heritage / Urban Ethics
Abstract re-ordering of sites can conflict with historic fabric, community continuity, ecology or displacement concerns. Existing conditions are not empty ground.

## Correction Rule
Historical evidence is a source of judgement mechanisms, never an exemption from contemporary requirements.
