# AR77-006 Sou Fujimoto｜Runtime Design DNA

Source basis: original 006 Skill.

Select **no more than 3**.

---

## DNA-01｜把边界设计成梯度

**Problem**  
Openness / shelter and public / private are often reduced to one wall or door.

**Principle**  
Boundary is a relational thickness, not a binary switch.

**Project operation**  
Combine shells, corridors, courts, visual filtering, semi-outdoor layers, acoustic and climate buffers.

**Spatial result**  
Users choose degrees of distance and exposure.

**Risk / correction**  
Visual transparency cannot replace thermal, privacy, security, and acoustic performance.

---

## DNA-02｜先提供可供性，再命名房间

**Problem**  
Fixed room names can lock behavior too early.

**Principle**  
Space may first offer sitting, leaning, climbing, viewing, gathering, or retreating possibilities.

**Project operation**  
Use dimensions, module, level, edges, platforms, and light to support multiple occupations.

**Spatial result**  
One position can support different activities at different times.

**Risk / correction**  
Healthcare, school, housing, and managed buildings still require explicit safety, privacy, equipment, and operations.

---

## DNA-03｜用剖面组织关系距离

**Problem**  
Plan zoning alone cannot fully express bodily, visual, and social distance.

**Principle**  
Height and visibility can create proximity, separation, observation, and participation.

**Project operation**  
Test continuous section, platforms, steps, voids, low/high ceilings, then return to plan.

**Spatial result**  
Limited area gains multiple relational positions.

**Risk / correction**  
Verify accessibility, fall protection, egress, cleaning, and furniture use.

---

## DNA-04｜用多中心场替代单一大厅

**Problem**  
One central hall can over-unify complex collective life.

**Principle**  
Multiple approximate centers can support selective participation.

**Project operation**  
Split volume or program islands and connect them with short paths, courts, sightlines, and shared boundaries.

**Spatial result**  
The project can combine domestic intimacy with urban-like variety.

**Risk / correction**  
Distributed centers must obey a clear wayfinding, supervision, service, and fire backbone.

---

## DNA-05｜让构件角色保持开放

**Problem**  
Separating every element into column, wall, slab, furniture can lose composite potential.

**Principle**  
A component may perform multiple roles depending on position and scale.

**Project operation**  
Choose a main module and test its roles in structure, furniture, boundary, path, and surface.

**Spatial result**  
Construction logic and occupation develop together.

**Risk / correction**  
Each performance must still be independently verified.

---

## DNA-06｜以密度和孔隙调节关系

**Problem**  
Pure openness or pure closure cannot answer complex environments.

**Principle**  
Density, porosity, openings, and layers can continuously adjust view, light, wind, sound, and social contact.

**Project operation**  
Create a density map: open / filtered / buffered / closed.

**Spatial result**  
Architecture becomes an adjustable relational field.

**Risk / correction**  
Visual lightness may carry high structural, thermal, and maintenance cost.

---

## DNA-07｜让自然参与运行，而不是装饰立面

**Problem**  
Nature is often reduced to tree shapes, green images, or branding.

**Principle**  
Nature must enter operation through climate, path, time, planting, and bodily experience.

**Project operation**  
Write tree retention, shade, ventilation, rainwater, seasonal use, outdoor activity, and public path into performance requirements.

**Spatial result**  
Built and natural systems exchange in actual use.

**Risk / correction**  
“Forest feeling” is not ecological proof.

---

## DNA-08｜设计可发现的路径，而非强迫的奇观

**Problem**  
Free circulation can become maze; strong axes can suppress discovery.

**Principle**  
Clear primary order and exploratory branches should coexist.

**Project operation**  
Provide legible main route, shortcuts, return views, pause nodes, and clear exits.

**Spatial result**  
Users can arrive efficiently and also discover.

**Risk / correction**  
Do not sacrifice accessibility, fire safety, night safety, child supervision, or operations.

---

# Selection rule

- max 3 DNA;
- every DNA must answer a project conflict;
- every DNA must be provable through plan / section / path evidence;
- delete any DNA without spatial evidence;
- no DNA may be selected because it “looks like Fujimoto”.
