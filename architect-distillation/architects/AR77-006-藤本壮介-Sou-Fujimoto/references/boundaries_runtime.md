# AR77-006｜Runtime Boundaries

## Climate & energy
Open and transparent historical experiments cannot be transplanted directly to hot-humid or extreme climates.

## Fire
Multi-level platforms, grids, complex openings, and multiple paths can complicate:
- fall protection;
- smoke control;
- egress;
- rescue.

## Structure & durability
Light appearance may depend on highly precise engineering.

## Privacy / acoustics / psychological safety
Mutual visibility is not automatically community.

## Accessibility
Every level difference must have an equivalent accessible route where required.

## Operations
Multiple centers and loops can complicate:
- navigation;
- cleaning;
- ticketing;
- supervision;
- night closure.

## Ecology
Tree / cloud / forest imagery is not environmental proof.

## Authorship
Collaborative projects must retain collaborator attribution.

## Professional verification
Do not make final determinations for:
- structure;
- MEP;
- fire;
- code;
- accessibility;
- acoustics;
- environmental performance;
- cost;
- construction;
- procurement.
