# AR77-012｜METHOD RUNTIME

## Evidence Status

### KNOWN
Authoritative Frank Lloyd Wright Foundation, UNESCO and U.S. National Park Service material supports these recurring ideas:
- Wright's “organic architecture” treated building, setting and relevant interior elements as an integrated whole.
- The Foundation describes the organic building as developing from within outward and being in harmony with time, place and inhabitants.
- UNESCO/NPS identify open plan, blurred exterior/interior boundaries, new uses of materials/technologies and explicit responses to suburban/natural settings as recurring qualities.
- UNESCO describes geometric abstraction and spatial manipulation responding to functional and emotional needs.
- The Foundation describes Wright's Usonian work as simplified residential construction responding to economic realities and changing social trends, with standardization used to support accessible tailoring.
- Wright's work addressed different needs across housing, worship, work, education and leisure.

### SUPPORTED INTERPRETATION
For contemporary project runtime, these recurring judgements can be translated into:
1. read life + place before image;
2. organize spatial continuity while retaining necessary boundaries;
3. use ordering geometry to coordinate parts rather than decorate;
4. make plan, section, movement, enclosure, structure, light and environment mutually reinforcing;
5. judge a part by whether it strengthens the whole;
6. let construction/environmental means vary with verified conditions rather than freeze historical Wright forms or palettes.

## Causal vs Appearance

Transferable:
- integrated whole;
- inside-out generation;
- spatial continuity;
- place response;
- ordering geometry;
- whole–part coordination;
- economy/repeatability as a design problem;
- structural/light/environmental integration when project evidence supports it.

Not default-transferable:
- low horizontal roof;
- cantilever;
- masonry hearth;
- art glass;
- textile block;
- desert masonry;
- spiral ramp;
- dendriform column;
- a fixed “natural material” palette.

## Evidence Boundary

Wright did not use the AR77 Route names, DNA names, 3–5 selection rule or A/B/C schema. These are operational distillation devices, not historical terminology.
