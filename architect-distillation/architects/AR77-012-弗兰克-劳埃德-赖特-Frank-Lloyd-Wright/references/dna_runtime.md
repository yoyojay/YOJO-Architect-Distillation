# AR77-012｜DESIGN DNA RUNTIME

> DNA = distilled decision mechanism, never a motif list.

## D1｜PLACE RECIPROCITY
**Judgement Meaning:** architecture should strengthen a specific place rather than dominate it.
**Trigger:** terrain/vegetation/water/horizon/climate/existing fabric materially shapes occupation.
**Spatial Operation:** position, section and thresholds respond to verified site forces; preserve decisive site character.
**Boundary:** ecology, grading, hazard, drainage, accessibility.
**Failure Mode:** picturesque “nature integration” with no measurable site logic.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** site/section shows what is preserved, touched, opened, shaded, drained or connected.

## D2｜INSIDE–OUTSIDE CONTINUITY
**Judgement Meaning:** boundary can connect occupation to exterior conditions without simply disappearing.
**Trigger:** outdoor use, view, daylight, ventilation or landscape relationship matters.
**Spatial Operation:** align rooms, thresholds, sheltered intermediate zones and exterior occupation.
**Boundary:** heat, glare, rain, privacy, security, acoustics.
**Failure Mode:** indiscriminate glazing or copied terraces.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** plan/section/path shows a calibrated gradient rather than binary wall/no-wall.

## D3｜CONTINUOUS ROOM FIELD
**Judgement Meaning:** remove unnecessary box separation so related activities form a coherent field.
**Trigger:** social/collective uses benefit from visual and movement continuity.
**Spatial Operation:** connect adjacent uses through shared volume, offset planes, partial boundaries or a programmatically justified shared anchor.
**Boundary:** fire, acoustics, privacy, infection-control, operations.
**Failure Mode:** undifferentiated/noisy open plan.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** room relations and circulation do not depend on residual corridors alone.

## D4｜HUMAN-SCALE SEQUENCE
**Judgement Meaning:** bodily movement reads shelter, release and changing orientation.
**Trigger:** arrival, transition, gathering or refuge needs experiential hierarchy.
**Spatial Operation:** calibrate approach, threshold, compression/release, sightline and destination.
**Boundary:** accessibility, crowd flow, headroom, safety.
**Failure Mode:** theatrical compression or maze-like movement.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** section/path storyboard proves sequence without iconic forms.

## D5｜ORDERING GEOMETRY
**Judgement Meaning:** geometry should coordinate decisions across scales.
**Trigger:** complex program, repetition, structural coordination or non-orthogonal site conditions.
**Spatial Operation:** establish a regulating module/grid/geometry organizing plan, structure, openings and elements.
**Boundary:** geometry must serve use and construction.
**Failure Mode:** decorative pattern or forced Wright-like geometry.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** one diagram explains recurring dimensional/relational order.

## D6｜WHOLE–PART INTEGRATION
**Judgement Meaning:** architecture, fixed interior elements and systems reinforce one coherent idea.
**Trigger:** experience depends on coordination across architecture/interior/system boundaries.
**Spatial Operation:** align key built-ins, service zones, lighting, thresholds and spatial order.
**Boundary:** replacement, accessibility, procurement, loose-fit future use.
**Failure Mode:** total-design control makes adaptation impossible.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** component/system decisions trace to the same organizing logic.

## D7｜ENVIRONMENTAL SHELTER
**Judgement Meaning:** shelter should mediate climate as part of spatial form.
**Trigger:** solar exposure, rain, wind, thermal swings or outdoor living is consequential.
**Spatial Operation:** derive depth, orientation, protected edges and environmental gradients from verified climate.
**Boundary:** energy code, moisture, wildfire/flood, maintenance.
**Failure Mode:** oversized Wright-like eaves with no performance basis.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** environmental section demonstrates shade/weather/comfort logic.

## D8｜STRUCTURE–LIGHT UNITY
**Judgement Meaning:** structure and light can jointly define collective space.
**Trigger:** large-span, workplace, civic or gallery space needs a legible spatial order.
**Spatial Operation:** coordinate structural rhythm, roof/ceiling zone, daylight and movement.
**Boundary:** engineering, glare, acoustics, MEP, maintenance.
**Failure Mode:** imitating dendriform columns or dramatic skylights.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** section/structural-light diagram shows one causal system.

## Selection Lock
Select 3–5 only. Every selected DNA must materially affect a drawing/test. Unselected DNA cannot re-enter Recommendation, Prompt or Handoff.
