# AR77-012｜BOUNDARIES + CONTEMPORARY CORRECTIONS

## Climate
Do not translate “organic” into generic openness. Verify orientation, solar gain, rain, wind, humidity, insulation, moisture, local hazards and passive comfort.

## Accessibility
Historical compression, level changes, narrow transitions or landscape routes are not defaults. Provide equivalent dignified accessible paths and inclusive experience.

## Fire
Continuous space must be reconciled with compartmentation, smoke control, travel distance, exits, occupant load and fire-service requirements.

## Structure
Cantilevers, unusual columns, thin edges or expressive spans are never assumed. Require current engineering, robustness, deflection and constructability checks.

## MEP
Whole–part integration must include maintainable service routes, plant access, replacement zones, controls and future upgrades.

## Acoustics
Open spatial continuity must be calibrated. Visual continuity does not require acoustic continuity.

## Privacy / Security
Boundary dissolution stops where privacy, safeguarding, security or controlled access requires separation.

## Operations
Test staffing, cleaning, deliveries, after-hours zoning, event changeover and independent operation.

## Maintenance
Integrated details and exposed weathering require drainage, inspection access, repair and replacement strategies.

## Cost
Bespoke integration is not automatically justified. Preserve judgement with simpler means where possible.

## Procurement
Test tolerances, trades, supply chain and replacement. Do not depend on unavailable craft merely to resemble historical work.

## Lifecycle
Test embodied/operational carbon, durability, adaptability and disassembly. Site integration must not become ecological disturbance.

## Historical Correction Rule
When a historically documented Wright mechanism conflicts with contemporary requirements, preserve the underlying judgement only if useful; redesign the means. Historical precedent is never a waiver.
