# AR77-012｜PROBLEM ROUTE RUNTIME

## R1｜SITE_AS_WHOLE
**Trigger:** terrain, vegetation, water, horizon, climate or existing natural character is a primary generator.
**Core Question:** how can occupation become part of the place rather than an object placed on it?
**Method:** preserve decisive verified site character → position/section by terrain and exposure → develop calibrated inside/outside thresholds → verify disturbance, climate and access.
**Primary DNA:** D1 / D2 / D5 / D7.
**Boundary:** ecology, grading, drainage, hazard and accessibility override picturesque integration.
**Risk:** romanticized “nature integration”; excessive disturbance; copied Fallingwater/Taliesin imagery.
**Difference:** site relation leads, rather than domestic social pattern or institutional field.

## R2｜DOMESTIC_CONTINUITY
**Trigger:** dwelling, hospitality, care or small-community program needs togetherness plus differentiated refuge.
**Core Question:** how can rooms become a connected field while retaining privacy and operational separation?
**Method:** define daily-life relationships/social anchor → remove unnecessary corridor/box separation → calibrate threshold, compression/release and refuge → verify acoustics, access, egress and change.
**Primary DNA:** D2 / D3 / D4 / D6.
**Boundary:** hearth/fireplace is never mandatory; contemporary social anchors may replace historical devices.
**Risk:** open-plan dogma; inaccessible compression; forced fireplace symbolism.
**Difference:** daily-life relations lead.

## R3｜CIVIC_INTERIOR
**Trigger:** worship, culture, learning or assembly needs a strong collective interior.
**Core Question:** how can sequence, light, structure and shared space form one civic experience?
**Method:** establish collective focus → choreograph approach/threshold → integrate structural/light order → maintain legible inclusive circulation.
**Primary DNA:** D3 / D4 / D5 / D8.
**Boundary:** exhibition, worship, education, crowd-flow and access requirements can limit continuity.
**Risk:** spectacle/iconic geometry dominating use.
**Difference:** collective interior experience leads.

## R4｜WORKING_COMMUNITY
**Trigger:** workplace/institution needs collective identity, environmental quality and legible organization.
**Core Question:** how can a large work field become socially coherent rather than a neutral container?
**Method:** define shared work/community field → coordinate structure, light and movement → locate services without breaking the primary order → test flexibility and future change.
**Primary DNA:** D3 / D5 / D6 / D8.
**Boundary:** acoustics, MEP, adaptability, operational churn.
**Risk:** over-integrated bespoke systems becoming inflexible.
**Difference:** organizational/technical field leads.

## R5｜ECONOMY_AND_REPEATABILITY
**Trigger:** housing/repeatable building must reconcile affordability with dignity, site specificity and spatial quality.
**Core Question:** what can be standardized without reducing life to a generic box?
**Method:** simplify program/construction → establish regulating module → concentrate infrastructure where appropriate → preserve meaningful social and environmental relations → test lifecycle cost/adaptability.
**Primary DNA:** D2 / D5 / D6 / D7.
**Boundary:** current code, labor, supply chain, maintenance and lifecycle economics.
**Risk:** treating “Usonian” as a formal template.
**Difference:** economy/repeatability leads.
