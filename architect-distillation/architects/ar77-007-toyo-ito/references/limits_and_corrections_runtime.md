# AR77-007｜Runtime Limits & Corrections

## L01｜Structural complexity
Integrated structure / space can increase analysis, tolerance, detailing, and construction risk.

**Correction:** involve qualified structural engineering early; retain alternatives, tolerances, and maintenance logic.

## L02｜Fire & egress
Open fields, cross-level networks, and curved spaces can weaken zoning and path readability.

**Correction:** keep primary egress clear; verify smoke, capacity, compartmentation, and fire systems.

## L03｜Accessibility
Slopes, complex networks, and multi-level connections may exclude some bodies.

**Correction:** provide equivalent accessible core experience and verify slope, rest, signage, and sanitary access.

## L04｜Acoustics / program conflict
Loose program boundaries can create mutual interference.

**Correction:** acoustic zoning, time zoning, closable edges, and event-mode planning.

## L05｜Climate / energy
Transparent skin, large openings, and large public volumes may create heat, glare, and energy burden.

**Correction:** redesign shading, ventilation, envelope, systems, and controls for local climate.

## L06｜Maintenance / system coupling
Highly synthesized structure, skin, services, and space can increase maintenance coupling.

**Correction:** provide maintenance paths, redundancy, replaceable units, and lifecycle budget.

## L07｜Complex geometry / cost
Curved walls and nonstandard nodes can lose cost control.

**Correction:** simplify rules, standardize components where possible, prototype critical nodes, and use cost gates.

## L08｜Media obsolescence
Fixed digital systems can age quickly.

**Correction:** keep networks and interfaces upgradeable and preserve low-tech equivalent use.

## L09｜Participation authenticity
“Open” and “community” can become empty claims.

**Correction:** record stakeholders, operator, decision rights, budget, and post-occupancy evaluation.

## L10｜Collaboration attribution
Major works depend on structural engineers, consultants, co-designers, and public institutions.

**Correction:** do not present technical achievement as a lone-author myth.

## L11｜Image-style bias
Tubes, branches, arches, caves, and glowing cylinders can overpower the method.

**Correction:** anti-icon audit is mandatory.

## L12｜Post-disaster / community ethics
Home-for-All must not become a cozy-hut template.

**Correction:** prioritize real community need, operation burden, long-term use, and shared decisions.

---

# Critical Fail

Recommendation is blocked if any occur:

- “Toyo Ito style” used as the main visual instruction;
- copying Sendai tubes, TOD'S branch façade, Tama arches, Taichung cave walls, Tower of Winds cylinder, or Home-for-All hut image;
- only renderings, no plan / section / system evidence;
- public openness without fire / accessibility / acoustic / operations correction;
- “natural / fluid” used as an untestable universal reason;
- technical collaboration erased;
- protocol-generated status presented as real project execution.
