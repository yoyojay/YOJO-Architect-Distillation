# AR77-007｜Period / Task Routes Runtime

These route names are AR77 runtime inferences derived from Toyo Ito works and texts. They are **not Toyo Ito's own historical period labels**.

---

## R01｜LIGHT_MEDIA_SKIN｜轻介质皮肤

### Typical task
Lightness, temporariness, city information, responsive envelope, physical/media overlap.

### Core judgement
Let enclosure mediate light, wind, urban information, and changing conditions instead of becoming a heavy permanent image.

### Strong evidence family
White U, Silver Hut, Tower of Winds, simulated-city writings.

### Required outputs
- environmental input diagram;
- skin response table;
- light-structure logic;
- day/night states;
- physical/media overlap diagram.

### Do not misuse
Do not copy metallic, silver, glowing-cylinder, or semi-transparent imagery.

---

## R02｜OPEN_INFRASTRUCTURE_FIELD｜开放基础设施场

### Typical task
Public cultural / information infrastructure with changing activities.

### Core judgement
Bring structure, vertical circulation, MEP, and media infrastructure forward as organizers of open space.

### Strong evidence family
Sendai Mediatheque.

### Required outputs
- infrastructure section;
- open plan;
- vertical-system diagram;
- activity-change diagram;
- operations + accessibility matrix.

### Do not misuse
Do not copy 13 tubes. Openness does not erase fire, acoustics, or operations.

---

## R03｜STRUCTURE_SURFACE_ORDER｜结构表皮秩序

### Typical task
Projects that need to rethink conventional separation of column grid, wall, opening, and skin.

### Core judgement
Modify rules so structure, opening, boundary, and spatial experience are organized by one readable system.

### Strong evidence family
Serpentine Pavilion 2002, TOD'S Omotesando, Tama Art University Library.

### Required outputs
- generative rule;
- structural logic;
- opening / structure conflict table;
- boundary section;
- terrain / site relationship.

### Do not misuse
Do not copy diagonal lattice, branch façade, or repeated arches.

---

## R04｜FLUID_NATURAL_NETWORK｜流动自然网络

### Typical task
Large cultural / public buildings with multi-level public flow and sensory continuity.

### Core judgement
Rebuild spatial networks from changing, flowing, heterogeneous relationships, integrating structure, environment, and sensory performance.

### Strong evidence family
National Taichung Theater and related Pritzker summaries.

### Required outputs
- topological network;
- sound / light / wind flows;
- structural-surface logic;
- fire zoning;
- public staying / activity network.

### Do not misuse
Do not use cave walls or organic surfaces as the answer.

---

## R05｜COMMUNAL_RESET｜共同体重置

### Typical task
Post-disaster, community, and public-responsibility projects.

### Core judgement
Ask why build, for whom, who decides, and who operates before form.

### Strong evidence family
Home-for-All and related social-responsibility work.

### Required outputs
- stakeholder map;
- decision-rights record;
- operator;
- adaptable-use plan;
- lifecycle / handover plan.

### Do not misuse
Do not copy hut aesthetics or use participation as a marketing label.

---

# Route Gate

1. One primary Route only.
2. One secondary Route only when a clearly separate secondary conflict exists.
3. R04 cannot replace R05's social process.
4. R01 media / skin experimentation does not automatically fit long-term public buildings.
5. R03 / R04 unconventional structures trigger structural, fire, cost, and buildability limits.
6. Visual similarity to a precedent is never a valid routing reason.
