# AR77-007｜Runtime Decision Rules

## DR01｜事实锁优先
Lock site, users, operations, climate, structure, fire, accessibility, media needs, and budget before form.

## DR02｜问题翻译成关系矛盾
Prefer fixed/flexible, grid/fluid, object/environment, physical/information, individual/community.

## DR03｜一主一路由
One primary Route. A secondary Route must solve a distinct secondary conflict.

## DR04｜DNA 必须跨越空间与技术
Select 4–7 DNA including spatial organization, structure/infrastructure, and reality correction.

## DR05｜三策略因果不同
A modifies rules.
B reorganizes infrastructure.
C rebuilds public / natural networks.

## DR06｜平面 + 剖面 + 系统证据
Every strategy requires plan, section, and structure / system evidence.

## DR07｜感官语言转性能
Translate light, fluid, natural, and open into daylight, ventilation, acoustics, thermal, circulation, variability, or structural criteria.

## DR08｜公共项目做运营测试
Test day/night, peak/normal, open/closed, event changes, accessibility, and fire.

## DR09｜执行反图标审计
Check tubes, branch façades, repeated arches, cave walls, glowing cylinders, and hut aesthetics.

## DR10｜来源与推论分层
Do not mix architect statements, project facts, institutional interpretation, AR77 inference, and real project testing.
