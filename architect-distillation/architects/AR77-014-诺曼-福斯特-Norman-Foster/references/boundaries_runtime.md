# AR77-014｜BOUNDARIES + CONTEMPORARY CORRECTIONS

## Climate
Use verified weather data. Check overheating, humidity, solar gain, glare, wind, rain, local hazards and future climate scenarios.

## Accessibility
Technical clarity must include inclusive routes, equivalent entrances, vertical circulation, wayfinding and dignified shared-space access.

## Fire
Atrium/interconnection, long spans, exposed structure and integrated systems require compartmentation, smoke control, protection, egress and fire-service verification.

## Structure
Efficiency claims require actual load paths, robustness, vibration, deflection, fire, connections, erection and embodied-carbon analysis.

## MEP
Integrate distribution, plant, controls, access, replacement and commissioning. Visible services are not inherently better services.

## Acoustics
Shared/large spaces and lightweight systems require acoustic separation, reverberation and services-noise checks.

## Privacy / Security
Transparency and connectivity stop where confidentiality, safeguarding, security or controlled access requires separation.

## Operations
Test occupancy schedules, cleaning, security, after-hours zoning, loading, event peaks, controls and actual user behavior.

## Maintenance
Check access, cleaning, replacement, façade seals, shading devices, sensors, controls and specialist components.

## Cost
Compare whole-life value, not only initial capital cost. Integration must not become unnecessary bespoke complexity.

## Procurement
Verify supply chain, tolerances, contractor capability, interfaces, commissioning and replacement availability.

## Lifecycle
Check embodied carbon, operational energy, durability, adaptability, disassembly, future climate and end-of-life scenarios.

## Performance Gap
Designed performance is not operational performance. Include commissioning, controls, handover and post-occupancy feedback where possible.

## Historical Correction Rule
When a documented Foster mechanism conflicts with contemporary requirements, preserve the underlying judgement only if useful; redesign the means. Historical precedent is never a waiver.
