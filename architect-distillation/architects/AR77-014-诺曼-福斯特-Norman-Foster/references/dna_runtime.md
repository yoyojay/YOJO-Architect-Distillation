# AR77-014｜DESIGN DNA RUNTIME

> DNA = distilled decision mechanism, never a motif list.

## D1｜PERFORMANCE BEFORE IMAGE
**Judgement Meaning:** measurable purpose and performance precede technological appearance.
**Trigger:** sustainability, comfort, operational or resource targets matter.
**Spatial Operation:** define targets and convert them into spatial/envelope/system requirements before form.
**Boundary:** metrics must be relevant and verifiable.
**Failure Mode:** sustainability claims used as image rhetoric.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** performance target → architectural response → test result.

## D2｜INTEGRATED SYSTEMS
**Judgement Meaning:** architecture, structure, envelope and services can form one coordinated order.
**Trigger:** technical systems materially shape space.
**Spatial Operation:** align grids/zones/voids/access and interfaces early.
**Boundary:** replacement, fire, acoustics, responsibility, procurement.
**Failure Mode:** exposed systems used decoratively.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** coordinated plan/section shows fewer system conflicts.

## D3｜STRUCTURAL ECONOMY
**Judgement Meaning:** structural means should deliver span, stability and spatial freedom with disciplined resource use.
**Trigger:** span, weight, repetition, carbon or construction economy is consequential.
**Spatial Operation:** compare structural typologies and grids against spatial/adaptability/material goals.
**Boundary:** robustness, vibration, fire, deflection, supply chain.
**Failure Mode:** diagrid/truss selected for Foster-like expression.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** load path/material quantity/space consequence can be compared.

## D4｜ENVIRONMENTAL MEDIATION
**Judgement Meaning:** envelope and section mediate climate rather than merely display transparency.
**Trigger:** sun, daylight, ventilation, heat, glare or seasonal comfort matters.
**Spatial Operation:** calibrate orientation, depth, shading, thermal zones, openings and systems from verified climate.
**Boundary:** acoustics, air quality, fire, security, controls.
**Failure Mode:** glass-heavy “eco-tech” image without performance.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** climate/daylight/energy test maps to section/envelope decisions.

## D5｜LONG-LIFE ADAPTABILITY
**Judgement Meaning:** long-lived architecture should accommodate foreseeable change.
**Trigger:** changing tenancy, technology, organization or program.
**Spatial Operation:** distinguish stable frame/core/infrastructure from replaceable partitions/services/fit-out.
**Boundary:** oversizing, embodied carbon, lease depth, fire/acoustics.
**Failure Mode:** generic flexibility with no change scenarios.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** at least two future scenarios work without destroying primary systems.

## D6｜LEGIBLE MOVEMENT
**Judgement Meaning:** circulation can clarify large/complex buildings and connect communities.
**Trigger:** high flows, public access, interchanges or multi-level organization.
**Spatial Operation:** create direct paths, visible destinations, shared nodes and intelligible vertical circulation.
**Boundary:** security, egress, accessibility, crowding.
**Failure Mode:** dramatic circulation that lengthens real journeys.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** route diagram proves clarity and conflict reduction.

## D7｜LIGHT + SHARED SPACE
**Judgement Meaning:** daylight and shared volume can support orientation, community and environmental quality.
**Trigger:** deep plans, collective programs or public interiors.
**Spatial Operation:** use section/voids/edges to distribute light and connect shared space where performance supports it.
**Boundary:** overheating, glare, acoustics, smoke, energy.
**Failure Mode:** giant atrium as signature gesture.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** daylight + movement + program diagrams align.

## D8｜RESEARCH + PROTOTYPE
**Judgement Meaning:** uncertain technical or environmental assumptions should be tested rather than stylized.
**Trigger:** novel system, material, geometry, environmental target or operational claim.
**Spatial Operation:** research precedent/data → model/simulate/mock up → compare → revise.
**Boundary:** test fidelity and criteria must match risk.
**Failure Mode:** technology demonstration without project value.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** test result changes a decision.

## Selection Lock

Select 3–5 only. Every selected DNA must materially affect a drawing, model, simulation or test. Unselected DNA cannot re-enter Recommendation, Prompt or Handoff.
