# AR77-001 Le Corbusier｜Runtime Design DNA

Source basis: the user's existing 001 Skill package.

Select **4–7** items per project.

---

## DNA-01｜结构先释放空间

**Problem**  
Does the load-bearing system lock the space too early?

**Principle**  
Dom-Ino and column-slab thinking separate structure from partitions.

**Project operation**  
Establish a rational structural field, core, or spanning logic before final room division.

**Expected result**  
Plan can change with use instead of being predetermined by bearing walls.

**Risk / correction**  
Freedom must not ignore lateral stability, services, acoustics, fire, construction, or economy.

---

## DNA-02｜把地面还给流动、景观或公共性

**Problem**  
Does the building unnecessarily block the site?

**Principle**  
Lifting is useful only when it releases ground for circulation, landscape, climate, or public use.

**Project operation**  
Use selective lifting, porosity, sheltered passage, or ground continuity only where project facts justify it.

**Expected result**  
Ground becomes active spatial infrastructure.

**Risk / correction**  
Avoid dark, windy, empty pilotis space with no program.

---

## DNA-03｜自由平面作为生活关系编辑器

**Problem**  
Do future activities require change?

**Principle**  
When structure and partition are separated, plan can follow activity, view, furniture, services, and time.

**Project operation**  
Use stable cores / service bands with adaptable occupied zones.

**Expected result**  
Clear but non-rigid spatial continuity.

**Risk / correction**  
Open plan must not destroy privacy, acoustics, or operations.

---

## DNA-04｜立面是结构之外的气候与观看界面

**Problem**  
Is façade driven only by structure or appearance?

**Principle**  
Openings and enclosure can be organized by light, view, climate, and internal activity.

**Project operation**  
Differentiate windows, deep openings, shading, solid zones, and ventilation by orientation and use.

**Expected result**  
Façade becomes a climate-and-view interface.

**Risk / correction**  
Ribbon windows are not universally appropriate. Verify heat gain, glare, rain, maintenance, and ventilation.

---

## DNA-05｜屋顶是被重新获得的地面

**Problem**  
Can occupied land be partially returned as usable roof?

**Principle**  
Roof may become a genuine layer of life.

**Project operation**  
Use roof for program, landscape, gathering, viewing, play, planting, or water management when operations support it.

**Expected result**  
Section gains an additional public or shared ground.

**Risk / correction**  
Verify load, waterproofing, wind, shade, safety, access, and management.

---

## DNA-06｜建筑漫游：用运动组织理解

**Problem**  
Is the building understood too quickly or only on plan?

**Principle**  
Architecture can unfold through movement and changing viewpoints.

**Project operation**  
Compose approach, compression, turn, rise, release, framed view, and return as needed.

**Expected result**  
Users understand the building through bodily sequence.

**Risk / correction**  
Ramps are not mandatory. Movement must balance experience, efficiency, and accessibility.

---

## DNA-07｜剖面制造关系，不只是叠楼层

**Problem**  
Are relationships limited to adjacency on plan?

**Principle**  
Double-height spaces, split levels, internal streets, duplexes, voids, and shared layers can build cross-level relationships.

**Project operation**  
Use section selectively to connect public/private, collective/individual, visible/accessible.

**Expected result**  
Spatial density and social relation increase.

**Risk / correction**  
Verify egress, structure, acoustics, accessibility, and service coordination.

---

## DNA-08｜个体单元与集体设施的平衡

**Problem**  
How can a project protect individual use while supporting common life?

**Principle**  
Individual units and shared facilities should be designed as one system.

**Project operation**  
First secure the quality of individual units, then position collective services where encounter and operation are plausible.

**Expected result**  
Community becomes layered rather than corridor + rooms.

**Risk / correction**  
Avoid megastructure overpowering street, neighborhood, or actual management.

---

## DNA-09｜人体比例作为校准工具，而非教条

**Problem**  
How can standardization remain connected to real bodies?

**Principle**  
Proportion is a calibration tool, not a universal body law.

**Project operation**  
Test reach, sitting, standing, turning, views, furniture, accessibility, children, elderly users, and wheelchair users.

**Expected result**  
Dimensions gain bodily logic.

**Risk / correction**  
Do not treat Modulor's historical male body as universal.

---

## DNA-10｜气候构件成为建筑深度

**Problem**  
How should sun, rain, heat, and glare become architecture?

**Principle**  
Deep façades, brise-soleil, galleries, and shelter can turn environmental response into occupiable depth.

**Project operation**  
Derive shading and depth from actual sun, rain, wind, and use.

**Expected result**  
Envelope gains shadow, orientation, depth, and inhabitation.

**Risk / correction**  
Do not copy Ahmedabad / Chandigarh climate devices without local validation.

---

## DNA-11｜材料表达制作过程

**Problem**  
Does material communicate construction or merely imitate an aesthetic?

**Principle**  
Material can reveal weight, joints, casting, assembly, touch, and time.

**Project operation**  
Let concrete, masonry, timber, metal, finish, color, or joints reflect plausible making.

**Expected result**  
Material expression becomes tectonic rather than decorative.

**Risk / correction**  
Roughness is not permission for poor workmanship or poor durability.

---

## DNA-12｜理性骨架与诗性触发物对位

**Problem**  
How can a strict system make room for site, emotion, ritual, light, or sound?

**Principle**  
Keep one clear order, then introduce a limited high-intensity deviation justified by site or perception.

**Project operation**  
Use one or very few spatial exceptions derived from horizon, light, sound, natural object, ritual, or landscape.

**Expected result**  
Poetry appears as tension within order.

**Risk / correction**  
Every curve, thick wall, void, or exceptional form must have a project-specific reason.

---

# Selection rule

For one project:

- select 4–7 DNA;
- each DNA must answer a concrete project problem;
- do not mechanically use the Five Points;
- do not treat iconic historical elements as mandatory.
