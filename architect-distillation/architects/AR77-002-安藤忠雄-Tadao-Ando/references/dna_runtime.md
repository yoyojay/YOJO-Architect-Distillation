# AR77-002 Tadao Ando｜Runtime Design DNA

Source basis: the user's existing 002 Skill package.

Select **4–7** items per project.

---

## DNA-01｜边界先行

**Problem**  
Does the project need protection from noise, visual clutter, exposure, or lack of focus?

**Principle**  
First establish a boundary, then selectively reconnect to the outside.

**Project operation**  
Use enclosure, layered edges, controlled openings, or landscape thresholds to filter the environment.

**Expected result**  
The interior gains concentration while specific relationships to outside remain intentional.

**Risk / correction**  
Inwardness must not damage safety, public accessibility, surveillance, ventilation, or operation.

---

## DNA-02｜墙体导向

**Problem**  
Is movement too direct to create orientation, transition, or perceptual focus?

**Principle**  
A wall can guide turning, slowing, and seeing, not only enclose.

**Project operation**  
Position walls to shape approach, threshold, turn, reveal, and pause.

**Expected result**  
Movement becomes spatially legible.

**Risk / correction**  
Avoid unnecessary barriers, inaccessible detours, or signature slanted walls without purpose.

---

## DNA-03｜必要绕行

**Problem**  
Would the shortest path erase transition, anticipation, or bodily awareness?

**Principle**  
Arrival can be prepared through movement.

**Project operation**  
Introduce a justified detour, change of level, turn, or threshold sequence.

**Expected result**  
Users experience a deliberate transition rather than instant arrival.

**Risk / correction**  
Detour must not compromise accessibility, emergency egress, daily efficiency, or dignity.

---

## DNA-04｜庭院插入天气

**Problem**  
Can light, wind, rain, and temperature become an intentional part of daily life?

**Principle**  
An open intermediate zone can place weather inside the spatial sequence.

**Project operation**  
Use courtyard, light well, void, outdoor threshold, or semi-open space where climate allows.

**Expected result**  
The body senses environmental change as part of architecture.

**Risk / correction**  
Provide drainage, anti-slip strategy, shade, ventilation, maintenance, and protected equivalent routes where needed.

---

## DNA-05｜光被切割

**Problem**  
Is light too uniform to structure attention, time, or ritual?

**Principle**  
Limited openings can give light direction and temporal depth.

**Project operation**  
Control size, orientation, depth, and sequence of openings.

**Expected result**  
Light helps organize perception rather than only illuminate.

**Risk / correction**  
Verify glare, heat gain, visual comfort, and exhibition protection.

---

## DNA-06｜压缩—释放

**Problem**  
Does the project lack spatial tension or threshold intensity?

**Principle**  
Narrow / low / dark conditions can prepare a later high / bright / open release.

**Project operation**  
Compose calibrated transitions in plan and section.

**Expected result**  
Spatial contrast becomes bodily legible.

**Risk / correction**  
Do not use compression in ways that feel unsafe, inaccessible, claustrophobic, or operationally inefficient.

---

## DNA-07｜纯几何建立秩序

**Problem**  
Does the site or program need a clear framework to control complexity?

**Principle**  
Square, circle, triangle, or other simple geometries can organize relationships.

**Project operation**  
Use one primary geometric order to discipline circulation, voids, program, or structure.

**Expected result**  
Complex conditions become readable.

**Risk / correction**  
Geometry is a control framework, not an icon. Do not force the site to serve a shape.

---

## DNA-08｜自然被框取

**Problem**  
Is landscape being treated as decoration rather than a perceptual relationship?

**Principle**  
Select what enters perception.

**Project operation**  
Frame sky, tree, water, slope, horizon, or a specific urban fragment through openings and path sequence.

**Expected result**  
Nature becomes precise and memorable.

**Risk / correction**  
Avoid turning every edge into a picture window. Maintain climate, privacy, and real use.

---

## DNA-09｜地形切入与隐藏

**Problem**  
Would a visible object overpower the site?

**Principle**  
Section can cut into, embed within, or follow terrain.

**Project operation**  
Use stepped levels, retaining edges, partial burial, terraces, or terrain-aligned movement.

**Expected result**  
Building mass becomes less dominant while section gains intensity.

**Risk / correction**  
Verify drainage, waterproofing, structure, accessibility, excavation, cost, and maintenance.

---

## DNA-10｜水面延长感知

**Problem**  
Can reflection, sound, distance, or boundary extend the perceptual field?

**Principle**  
Water can expand spatial perception beyond the building edge.

**Project operation**  
Use water only where site, climate, maintenance, safety, and program justify it.

**Expected result**  
Space gains reflection, distance, sound, or horizon.

**Risk / correction**  
Avoid ornamental water without maintenance, climate, safety, or ecological logic.

---

## DNA-11｜材料作为光的背景

**Problem**  
Is material being chosen as image rather than perceptual infrastructure?

**Principle**  
Material should support shadow, touch, scale, craft, and time.

**Project operation**  
Choose material according to local craft, climate, maintenance, carbon, budget, and desired light response.

**Expected result**  
Material reinforces atmosphere without becoming a stylistic label.

**Risk / correction**  
Fair-faced concrete is only one possible material. Never treat it as mandatory.

---

## DNA-12｜留白与静默

**Problem**  
Does the project need perceptual focus, art attention, ritual, or psychological distance from visual noise?

**Principle**  
Reduce competing stimuli.

**Project operation**  
Simplify views, signage, material palette, and spatial clutter around specific moments.

**Expected result**  
Attention shifts toward body, light, art, sound, weather, or ritual.

**Risk / correction**  
Silence must not become empty luxury minimalism, poor wayfinding, or social exclusion.

---

# Selection rule

For one project:

- select 4–7 DNA;
- each DNA must answer a concrete project fact or conflict;
- every DNA must include a risk / contemporary correction;
- do not select DNA because it “looks like Ando”.
