# AR77-011｜Boundaries + Contemporary Corrections Runtime

## Climate
Large envelopes, atria, deep plans, extensive glazing and complex sections require orientation, solar, daylight, overheating, condensation, ventilation and energy checks.

## Accessibility
Trajectory is never permission for forced detours, inaccessible slopes or unequal routes. Direct, dignified accessible movement is mandatory.

## Fire
Complex section, voids and interconnected floors may trigger compartmentation, smoke-control and egress challenges. Fire strategy can reshape the concept.

## Structure
Large voids, cantilevers, transfer systems and irregular loads require engineer verification. Never invent structural spectacle.

## MEP
Programmatic complexity creates zoning, plant, riser, maintenance and control complexity. Integrate early.

## Acoustics
Overlapping programs can conflict severely. Use actual sound profiles, not generic “vibrancy.”

## Privacy
Visual connection and social overlap must not erase confidential, domestic or care-related boundaries.

## Operations / Security
Test opening hours, ticketing, deliveries, staff routes, closures, crowd peaks, cleaning and emergency scenarios.

## Maintenance
Complex envelopes, elevated public routes and interior voids increase access, cleaning and replacement burdens.

## Cost / Procurement
Nonstandard geometry or systems must justify themselves through organizational performance, not image.

## Lifecycle
Test adaptability of program zones and service systems, not just initial novelty.

## Carbon
Large volume and high material intensity require embodied + operational carbon scrutiny.

## Heritage
Typology mutation in existing buildings must distinguish significance from ordinary fabric.

## Mobility / Urban Equity
Urban-scale concentration can affect access, displacement, transport capacity and public-space ownership.

## Correction Rule
Contemporary performance may limit or overturn an architect-derived mechanism. That is a valid result.
