# AR77-007 Toyo Ito｜Runtime Design DNA

Source basis: original 007 `design-dna.md`.

Select **4–7** DNA.

---

## D01｜CONDITION_BEFORE_STYLE｜条件先于风格

**Problem**  
A personal visual language is covering different project conditions.

**Principle**  
Site, commission, social conditions, and technical problems determine rules before style.

**Operation**  
Build fact lock + conflict matrix before choosing a spatial system.

**Result**  
Different projects may look different while judgement remains traceable.

**Risk**  
“Every project is different” can become arbitrary.

**Correction**  
Every difference must point back to users, structure, site, environment, or measurable performance.

---

## D02｜LOOSEN_PROGRAM_BORDERS｜放松功能边界

**Problem**  
Public buildings are fragmented by rigid departmental boundaries.

**Principle**  
Keep necessary identities but remove unnecessary partitions.

**Operation**  
Shared platforms, variable boundaries, time-based use, multi-media overlap.

**Result**  
Activities can migrate and recombine.

**Risk**  
Acoustics, safety, collections, and operations may conflict.

**Correction**  
Map fixed vs flexible boundaries and test day/night + peak conditions.

---

## D03｜INFRASTRUCTURE_BECOMES_SPACE｜基础设施成为空间

**Problem**  
Structure, transport, and MEP remain hidden while space becomes an empty shell.

**Principle**  
Infrastructure can organize public space.

**Operation**  
Use cores, shafts, structural grids, service bands, or environmental systems as spatial nodes.

**Result**  
Technical systems and public space become causally connected.

**Risk**  
Maintenance and system coupling become difficult.

**Correction**  
Provide role-conflict matrix, maintenance path, redundancy, and replaceability.

---

## D04｜MODIFY_THE_GRID｜修改网格而非抛弃秩序

**Problem**  
Standard grids homogenize site and behavior.

**Principle**  
Start from calculable order, then modify it through bending, shifting, density, or local exceptions.

**Operation**  
Parametric or rule-based variation linked to load, path, view, terrain, or environmental input.

**Result**  
Order remains buildable without becoming uniform.

**Risk**  
Complexity becomes pattern.

**Correction**  
Every deviation must answer a real input.

---

## D05｜STRUCTURE_SKIN_SPACE_SYNTHESIS｜结构·表皮·空间合成

**Problem**  
Structure, façade, opening, and interior are designed as unrelated systems.

**Principle**  
One generative logic may organize load, opening, skin, and spatial experience.

**Operation**  
Structure-skin integration, branching load paths, wall-bearing systems, structural openings.

**Result**  
Construction becomes part of spatial order.

**Risk**  
High image recognizability and engineering complexity.

**Correction**  
Performance comes first; never reverse-engineer a precedent's image.

---

## D06｜PERMEABLE_EDGE｜可渗透边界

**Problem**  
Inside/outside and public/restricted relations are treated as binary.

**Principle**  
Permeability can be tuned across visual, climatic, acoustic, and operational layers.

**Operation**  
Openings, shared ground, semi-outdoor zones, multiple entries, transparent or filtered edges.

**Result**  
Boundary becomes an exchange interface.

**Risk**  
Transparency does not equal openness.

**Correction**  
Draw visual, climate, acoustic, fire, security, and management boundaries separately.

---

## D07｜PHYSICAL_INFORMATION_DUAL_BODY｜物理与信息双重身体

**Problem**  
Digital media is isolated from bodily space.

**Principle**  
Support both physical bodies and information-network bodies.

**Operation**  
Coordinate data / media interfaces with real staying, sightlines, daylight, airflow, and non-screen alternatives.

**Result**  
Digital life and bodily life can coexist.

**Risk**  
May collapse into screens and tech UI.

**Correction**  
Digital systems must serve public use and retain low-tech equivalent experiences.

---

## D08｜SENSORY_FLOW｜感官流动

**Problem**  
Architecture focuses only on geometry and image.

**Principle**  
Light, air, water, sound, slope, body movement, and pause jointly construct space.

**Operation**  
Map sensory and bodily flows.

**Result**  
Architecture is experienced by the whole body.

**Risk**  
Poetic language can hide poor performance.

**Correction**  
Bind each sensory claim to a test or performance condition.

---

## D09｜NATURE_AS_DYNAMIC_PRINCIPLE｜自然作为动态原则

**Problem**  
Nature is reduced to tree-like columns or organic curves.

**Principle**  
Translate complexity, variation, flow, and heterogeneity rather than natural shapes.

**Operation**  
Use flow, density, networks, topography, and local difference as rules.

**Result**  
Environment becomes a dynamic relation.

**Risk**  
Easily becomes organic-style imagery.

**Correction**  
Delete all nature metaphors and check whether structure, environment, and use still explain the proposal.

---

## D10｜TOPOLOGICAL_CONNECTIONS｜拓扑连接优先

**Problem**  
Rooms are connected only by linear corridors and floor-by-floor logic.

**Principle**  
Prioritize how nodes connect, cross, see, and exchange.

**Operation**  
Horizontal / vertical networks, voids, cross-level relations, slopes, continuous public space.

**Result**  
Complex activity becomes a readable relationship network.

**Risk**  
Wayfinding, egress, and accessibility may suffer.

**Correction**  
Always draw main path, accessible path, fire path, and peak-flow test.

---

## D11｜LIGHTNESS_WITH_TECHNICAL_DEPTH｜轻盈必须有技术深度

**Problem**  
Lightness is reduced to thin, white, or transparent appearance.

**Principle**  
Real lightness comes from structural efficiency, environmental systems, boundary performance, and reducing unnecessary partitions.

**Operation**  
Efficient structure, double skin where justified, service integration, precise load path and detailing.

**Result**  
Open space with technical depth.

**Risk**  
Visual lightness can hide high energy, maintenance, or detailing burdens.

**Correction**  
Include structure, energy, maintenance, and lifecycle in early validation.

---

## D12｜COMMUNITY_BEFORE_ORIGINALITY｜共同体先于原创性

**Problem**  
Architectural originality overrides real community need.

**Principle**  
Ask why, for whom, and who maintains before personal expression.

**Operation**  
Stakeholder mapping, decision records, low-threshold gathering, operator definition, lifecycle transfer.

**Result**  
Architecture supports durable community use.

**Risk**  
Participation becomes symbolic.

**Correction**  
Record decision rights, budget, maintenance responsibility, and post-occupancy review.

---

# Combination discipline

- D01 is mandatory.
- Public buildings must include at least two of D02 / D03 / D10.
- Nature / organic arguments require D09.
- Lightness / transparency arguments require D11.
- Community / post-disaster projects require D12.
- Structure-integration proposals must trigger Limits review.
