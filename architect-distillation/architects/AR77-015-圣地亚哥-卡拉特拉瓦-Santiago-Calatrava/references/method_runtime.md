# AR77-015｜METHOD RUNTIME

## Evidence Status

### KNOWN

- Calatrava was formally trained in architecture and civil/structural engineering; his ETH doctoral thesis investigated foldability of space-frame structures.
- MoMA's 1993 exhibition explicitly framed his work around **Structure and Expression**, transformation and movement, and documented a process in which sketches of trees, animals and the human body were transformed into bridges, buildings and structures.
- MoMA also documented kinetic architecture in which doors, roofs and installations physically changed state, not merely appeared dynamic.
- The Metropolitan Museum documented a direct relationship between Calatrava's independent sculpture/drawing practice and architectural form.
- In a published interview on his official site, Calatrava described sketching as the first design step, followed by models or rough schematics and then increasingly collaborative/digital translation.
- Calatrava has explicitly described movement and dynamics as central interests, connected to changes in nature, sun, wind, functional need and mechanical solutions.
- Milwaukee Art Museum documents a genuinely movable brise soleil operating as both environmental device and civic event, showing that kinetic expression in his work can have functional/mechanical content rather than being purely visual.

### SUPPORTED INTERPRETATION

For contemporary project runtime, these recurring mechanisms can be translated into:
1. begin from human use/measure and context;
2. use drawing to discover force, movement and analogy;
3. abstract natural/anatomical sources into spatial/structural relations rather than literal motifs;
4. let structural force become spatial expression only when structurally real;
5. employ actual transformation only when it serves function, climate or experience;
6. coordinate plan, section and movement so expressive structures are inhabited;
7. move from sketch to model/schematic to verified engineering translation;
8. preserve emotional/civic intensity only when safety, weather, maintenance and constructability remain credible.

## Method Sequence

`HUMAN / USE / CONTEXT`

## Causal vs Appearance

Transferable:
- architecture + engineering integration;
- drawing-led discovery;
- natural/body analogy as abstraction;
- force made legible;
- structure-space unity;
- real kinetic transformation;
- movement through section;
- model/schematic development;
- collaborative technical translation;
- civic/emotional intensity grounded in function.

Not default-transferable:
- white structural palette;
- ribs/bones/wings;
- eye/bird/fish imagery;
- leaning masts;
- cable-stayed silhouettes;
- twisting towers;
- kinetic sunshades;
- skeletal vaults;
- any recognizable project geometry.
