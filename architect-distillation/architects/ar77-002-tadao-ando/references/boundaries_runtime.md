# AR77-002｜Runtime Boundaries

## Strong method questions

002 is useful when the project needs to examine:

- boundary and inwardness;
- indirect approach;
- bodily movement;
- courtyard / weather;
- directional light;
- compressed / released sequence;
- simple geometric order;
- selective framing of nature;
- topographic section;
- water as perceptual extension;
- material / light relation;
- visual quiet;
- adaptive reuse through precise insertion.

## Caution

### Weather exposure
Must not make basic use unsafe or undignified.

### Detour
Must not compromise accessibility, emergency movement, or normal operation.

### Inwardness
Must not erase community safety, natural surveillance, or public access.

### Natural light
Must be checked for glare, heat, exhibition protection, and comfort.

### Terrain cutting
Requires drainage, waterproofing, structure, accessibility, excavation, and cost verification.

### Water
Requires safety, maintenance, climate, ecological, and operational justification.

### Material
Do not assume fair-faced concrete is feasible, low-carbon, affordable, or maintainable.

### Minimalism
Do not turn “silence” into empty luxury space or poor wayfinding.

## Professional verification

Do not make final determinations for:

- structure;
- fire;
- code;
- MEP;
- drainage;
- accessibility;
- environmental performance;
- acoustics;
- cost;
- construction;
- procurement.
