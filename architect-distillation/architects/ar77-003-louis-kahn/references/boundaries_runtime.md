# AR77-003｜Runtime Boundaries

## Strong method questions

003 is useful when the project needs to examine:

- institutional purpose;
- room relationships;
- primary / service-space coordination;
- structure as spatial order;
- natural light by room type;
- public / collective core;
- threshold and institutional dignity;
- repeated room / structural units;
- stable order with future change;
- adaptive reuse of masonry / institutional fabric.

## Caution

### Laboratory / research
Historical served / service clarity does not automatically solve modern flexibility, HVAC, contamination control, or equipment needs.

### Natural light
Must be checked for:
- glare;
- heat;
- daylight distribution;
- exhibition protection;
- climate.

### Monumentality
Must not become inaccessible authority imagery.

### Heavy material
Brick, stone, and concrete can create carbon, cost, craft, and maintenance burdens.

### Repetition
Strong modules must not flatten room differences or prevent future adaptation.

### Public core
Visibility must be matched with universal access, safe egress, and real operating control.

### Existing buildings
New intervention must not erase the memory or structural reality of the existing fabric.

## Professional verification

Do not make final determinations for:

- structure;
- fire;
- code;
- MEP;
- accessibility;
- environmental performance;
- acoustics;
- cost;
- construction;
- procurement.
