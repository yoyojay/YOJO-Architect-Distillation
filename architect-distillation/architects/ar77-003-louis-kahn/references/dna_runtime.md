# AR77-003 Louis Kahn｜Runtime Design DNA

Source basis: the user's existing 003 Skill package.

Select **4–7** items per project.

---

## DNA-01｜机构先于造型

**Problem**  
Is form being chosen before the institution's purpose is understood?

**Principle**  
Define what the institution wants to make possible before choosing form.

**Project operation**  
Write the institutional wish, identify users and shared activities, then test which spatial relationships support it.

**Expected result**  
Form emerges from institutional purpose rather than visual reference.

**Risk / correction**  
Institutional rhetoric must still be checked against real daily operation and accessibility.

---

## DNA-02｜房间是起点

**Problem**  
Is the project being designed as an exterior object with leftover internal space?

**Principle**  
Begin with rooms as meaningful units of activity.

**Project operation**  
Define major room types by activity, proportion, adjacency, structure, and light.

**Expected result**  
Primary spaces gain clarity before the shell is determined.

**Risk / correction**  
Rooms must not become rigid historical types unable to adapt.

---

## DNA-03｜房间形成社会

**Problem**  
Are rooms only arranged along corridors?

**Principle**  
Rooms should form relationships with public cores, thresholds, routes, and outdoor rooms.

**Project operation**  
Organize clusters, shared centers, courts, thresholds, and visible relationships according to institutional life.

**Expected result**  
The building becomes a society of rooms rather than a corridor machine.

**Risk / correction**  
Hierarchy must not create exclusion or confusing access.

---

## DNA-04｜被服务 / 服务空间分层

**Problem**  
Are stairs, toilets, plant, shafts, storage, and maintenance fragmenting primary rooms?

**Principle**  
Primary spaces and service/support systems should be legible as different but coordinated orders.

**Project operation**  
Group service functions into bands, layers, towers, cores, thickened edges, or other maintainable systems.

**Expected result**  
Primary rooms remain spatially coherent while technical systems remain accessible.

**Risk / correction**  
Do not over-separate services at the expense of flexibility, efficiency, cost, or contemporary MEP coordination.

---

## DNA-05｜结构生成空间秩序

**Problem**  
Is structure being added after room planning?

**Principle**  
Structural unit and room order should explain one another.

**Project operation**  
Coordinate bay, span, room proportion, circulation, and load path.

**Expected result**  
Structure contributes to spatial legibility.

**Risk / correction**  
Architectural clarity does not substitute for structural engineering.

---

## DNA-06｜设备、交通与维护进入明确支持系统

**Problem**  
Are service systems hidden as leftover space?

**Principle**  
Technical infrastructure deserves deliberate spatial order.

**Project operation**  
Coordinate stairs, toilets, shafts, plant, storage, service access, and maintenance in defined support zones.

**Expected result**  
Technical systems become understandable and maintainable without dominating primary rooms.

**Risk / correction**  
Avoid oversized “service castles” that reduce usable space or adaptability.

---

## DNA-07｜自然光赋予房间不同性格

**Problem**  
Is daylight treated uniformly across all room types?

**Principle**  
Different activities deserve different light conditions.

**Project operation**  
Assign side light, top light, reflected light, filtered light, deeper shade, or controlled brightness according to activity.

**Expected result**  
Light reinforces room identity and use.

**Risk / correction**  
Verify heat gain, glare, daylight performance, art protection, climate, and user comfort.

---

## DNA-08｜重复结构 / 空间单元建立整体并允许差异

**Problem**  
Does the project need a strong overall order without making every room identical?

**Principle**  
Repeated structural-spatial units can establish unity while accommodating local differences.

**Project operation**  
Define a base module, then allow controlled variation for program, light, access, and context.

**Expected result**  
The whole is legible without flattening difference.

**Risk / correction**  
Repetition must not freeze future change or create monotonous use conditions.

---

## DNA-09｜公共核心可见且可达

**Problem**  
Is the collective center hidden, exclusive, or difficult to access?

**Principle**  
Important shared spaces should be legible and equitably reachable.

**Project operation**  
Position public core, stairs, common room, reading hall, assembly, court, or shared threshold where users can understand and reach it.

**Expected result**  
Institutional collective life becomes spatially evident.

**Risk / correction**  
Visibility does not replace universal accessibility, fire safety, or real operating control.

---

## DNA-10｜材料、接缝、受力与开口互相解释

**Problem**  
Is material being used as historic atmosphere?

**Principle**  
Material expression should connect construction, load, joint, opening, touch, and durability.

**Project operation**  
Coordinate opening depth, structural logic, joints, surface, and maintenance.

**Expected result**  
Material appears tectonic rather than decorative.

**Risk / correction**  
Brick, concrete, stone, or heavy mass are not mandatory. Check carbon, local craft, cost, and maintenance.

---

## DNA-11｜门槛、前院、廊与尺度变化建立尊严

**Problem**  
Does entry happen too abruptly, or does monumentality overwhelm everyday access?

**Principle**  
Institutional dignity can be created through sequence and threshold rather than oversized symbols.

**Project operation**  
Use forecourt, porch, arcade, lobby threshold, court, compression/release, or calibrated scale change.

**Expected result**  
Arrival gains meaning without relying on iconography.

**Risk / correction**  
Keep access inclusive, weather-protected where necessary, and proportionate to daily use.

---

## DNA-12｜稳定秩序容纳未来变化

**Problem**  
Can the building preserve long-term identity while programs change?

**Principle**  
Keep durable spatial order where valuable, allow occupation and partitions to evolve.

**Project operation**  
Stabilize structure, public relationships, service logic, and daylight framework; allow selected internal zones to adapt.

**Expected result**  
Institutional continuity without programmatic rigidity.

**Risk / correction**  
Do not confuse permanence with fixed room assignment or difficult servicing.

---

# Selection rule

For one project:

- select 4–7 DNA;
- each DNA must answer a concrete project problem;
- each must state an operation, spatial result, risk, and contemporary correction;
- do not select visual signatures as DNA.
