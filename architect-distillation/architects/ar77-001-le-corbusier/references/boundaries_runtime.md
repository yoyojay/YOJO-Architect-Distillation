# AR77-001｜Runtime Boundaries

## Strong method questions

001 is useful when the project needs to examine:

- structural freedom;
- plan adaptability;
- sectional relationships;
- promenade;
- ground continuity / lifting;
- collective facilities;
- roof as program;
- climate-depth envelope;
- human-scale calibration;
- rational order and controlled poetic exception;
- civic sequence.

## Caution

### Free plan
May conflict with privacy, acoustics, services, fire, or operations.

### Pilotis / lifted ground
May produce empty, dark, windy, unmanaged space.

### Roof use
Requires structural, waterproofing, safety, wind, access, shade, and management verification.

### Promenade
Must not damage accessibility, efficiency, or orientation.

### Deep climate devices
Must come from actual climate conditions.

### Material roughness
Must not become poor detailing or fake “brutalist” texture.

### Monumentality
Must be justified by civic / institutional purpose, not borrowed from historical imagery.

### Existing community / urban fabric
Do not import clearance-based tabula-rasa logic.

## Professional verification

Do not make final determinations for:

- structure;
- fire;
- code;
- MEP;
- environmental performance;
- acoustics;
- cost;
- construction;
- procurement.
