# AR77-016｜DESIGN DNA RUNTIME

> DNA = distilled decision mechanism, never a motif list.

## D1｜POSITION BEFORE STYLE
**Judgement Meaning:** define the project's architectural/cultural stance before choosing formal language.
**Trigger:** institution, heritage or strong context demands a clear position.
**Spatial Operation:** state what the project aligns with, challenges or reframes, then derive spatial consequences.
**Boundary:** position must answer project facts, not fashion.
**Failure Mode:** arbitrary stylistic switching.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** stated position maps to plan/threshold/context decisions.

## D2｜PRECEDENT AS ARGUMENT
**Judgement Meaning:** precedent is evidence to reinterpret, not a form to reproduce.
**Trigger:** historical/typological reference is relevant.
**Spatial Operation:** extract a principle—order, boundary, procession, contrast—and transform it.
**Boundary:** cultural/heritage accuracy.
**Failure Mode:** copied pediment, glass box or motif.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** precedent → abstract principle → changed project operation.

## D3｜PRIMARY GEOMETRIC ORDER
**Judgement Meaning:** a strong primary order can clarify space and relationships.
**Trigger:** compact program or institutional composition needs legibility.
**Spatial Operation:** establish grid/axis/volume/field before secondary exceptions.
**Boundary:** program, structure, climate and adaptability can override purity.
**Failure Mode:** geometric purity forcing bad use.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** plan/section remain intelligible after removing decorative language.

## D4｜FRAME + THRESHOLD
**Judgement Meaning:** boundaries can deliberately frame entry, view, privacy and transition.
**Trigger:** public/private, inside/outside or old/new relations matter.
**Spatial Operation:** calibrate enclosure, opening, vestibule, portal, edge and view.
**Boundary:** climate, privacy, accessibility, fire.
**Failure Mode:** theatrical framing with poor environmental performance.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** threshold diagram shows changed visibility/access/experience.

## D5｜OBJECT / SITE TENSION
**Judgement Meaning:** a distinct object can gain meaning through deliberate relation to landscape or context.
**Trigger:** strong site/campus/urban counterpart exists.
**Spatial Operation:** align, offset, contrast or pair object with contextual orders.
**Boundary:** scale, public realm, heritage, ecology.
**Failure Mode:** autonomous icon indifferent to surroundings.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** site plan proves reciprocal relation.

## D6｜CONTROLLED SEQUENCE
**Judgement Meaning:** approach and procession can structure perception over time.
**Trigger:** arrival or spatial reveal is consequential.
**Spatial Operation:** sequence compression/release, turn, pause, framed view and destination.
**Boundary:** directness, accessibility, egress.
**Failure Mode:** ceremonial detour imposed on routine use.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** path storyboard explains each transition.

## D7｜CONTRAST AS CLARIFIER
**Judgement Meaning:** contrast can make old/new, solid/void or public/private relations more legible.
**Trigger:** two conditions need distinction.
**Spatial Operation:** contrast scale, boundary, geometry or spatial density only where it clarifies the relationship.
**Boundary:** no arbitrary visual shock.
**Failure Mode:** novelty for novelty's sake.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** removing contrast makes the key relation less legible.

## D8｜REVISION OF POSITION
**Judgement Meaning:** architectural commitments may need revision when cultural/project evidence changes.
**Trigger:** inherited concept no longer serves current facts.
**Spatial Operation:** identify obsolete assumption → retain useful mechanism → replace its expression/operation.
**Boundary:** revision requires reason, not trend-chasing.
**Failure Mode:** eclecticism without judgement.
**Verifiable Spatial Trace:** before/after decision map shows what evidence changed.

## Selection Lock

Select 3–5 only. Every selected DNA must materially affect a drawing, model, comparison or test. Unselected DNA cannot re-enter Recommendation, Prompt or Handoff.
