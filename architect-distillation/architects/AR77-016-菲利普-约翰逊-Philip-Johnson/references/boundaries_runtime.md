# AR77-016｜BOUNDARIES + CONTEMPORARY CORRECTIONS

## Climate
Transparent/minimal envelopes require verified solar gain, glare, heat loss/gain, condensation, shading and future-climate checks.

## Accessibility
Procession, podiums, level changes and monumental thresholds require equivalent dignified step-free routes and clear wayfinding.

## Fire
Open/transparent volumes, atria and unusual thresholds require egress, smoke, compartmentation and structural fire review.

## Structure
Strong grids/volumes must correspond to real spans, loads, robustness and adaptability rather than graphic order alone.

## MEP
Minimal/monumental spaces still require plant, distribution, drainage, controls, access and replacement.

## Acoustics
Hard transparent/stone-like public spaces require reverberation, privacy and services-noise checks.

## Privacy
Transparency is never a default virtue. Test visual privacy, security, nighttime use, domestic dignity and confidential functions.

## Operations
Test everyday entrances, staff/service routes, event mode, cleaning, security and after-hours zoning.

## Maintenance
Check glass/metal/stone access, seal replacement, cleaning, drainage, joints and specialist ornament/components.

## Cost
Compare representational complexity and bespoke detailing against actual institutional/user value.

## Procurement
Historical/custom geometries require fabrication capability, tolerances, samples, interfaces and replacement strategy.

## Lifecycle
Check embodied carbon, operational energy, durability, adaptability and future reuse.

## Social / Institutional Correction
Architectural identity must be tested against who is represented, who is excluded, and whether monumental symbolism conflicts with contemporary institutional values.

## Heritage Correction
Precedent and contrast require verified heritage significance and planning constraints. Historical reference is not automatically contextual.

## Historical Correction Rule
When a documented Johnson mechanism conflicts with contemporary requirements, preserve the underlying judgement only if useful; redesign the means. Historical precedent is never a waiver.
