# AR77-006｜Period Routes Runtime

Source basis: the original 006 Skill.

---

## R1｜松散聚落 Route

### Trigger
When the project needs care, solitude, observation, and collective life at the same time.

### Suitable
Children, rehabilitation, education, community, mixed public projects.

### Not suitable
Areas requiring highly centralized security, single clean/dirty process, or strict axial control.

### Core judgement
Break the whole into identifiable small units and use multiple approximate centers, short paths, and distance differences to support selective participation.

### Conflict rule
If distributed centers damage supervision, legibility, or egress, first preserve a clear safety backbone, then layer the loose field above it.

---

## R2｜领域渐变 Route

### Trigger
When the main conflict is public/private, inside/outside, openness/shelter.

### Suitable
Housing, community fronts, cultural boundaries, campuses, mixed public space.

### Not suitable
High pollution, high noise, extreme climate, or high-security boundaries.

### Core judgement
Do not rely on one wall. Use shells, courts, corridors, vegetation, visual filtering, and thresholds to create multiple distances.

### Conflict rule
When climate and safety demands are high, relationship gradient and physical-performance layer must be designed separately.

---

## R3｜未分化构件 Route

### Trigger
When the project wants fewer component categories and a main component / module to take multiple roles.

### Suitable
Small experimental spaces, pavilions, furniture-like architecture, children's / cultural installations.

### Not suitable
High-rise, hospitals, complex MEP, strict fire compartmentation, highly variable leasing.

### Core judgement
Choose one main component or module and test how it can act as structure, surface, furniture, path, and boundary.

### Conflict rule
Composite role cannot hide independent verification of structure, fire, waterproofing, insulation, maintenance, and ergonomics.

---

## R4｜剖面地形 Route

### Trigger
When plan area is limited but activity relations need height, sightline, and bodily distance.

### Suitable
Small houses, studios, reading spaces, exhibitions, children's spaces.

### Not suitable
High accessibility demand, strict egress, long-term elderly use, or limited floor-to-floor height.

### Core judgement
Use platforms, steps, level shifts, voids, and visibility to create different occupation positions before dividing into conventional rooms.

### Conflict rule
Establish a continuous accessible main path and fall-protection system before optional sectional differences.

---

## R5｜搜索与偶遇 Route

### Trigger
When the project needs both efficient orientation and unplanned discovery.

### Suitable
Libraries, exhibitions, schools, innovation centers, public learning spaces.

### Not suitable
High-efficiency logistics, emergency medical flow, pure backstage production.

### Core judgement
Create an understandable main order, then add perforations, shortcuts, sightlines, loops, and local nodes.

### Conflict rule
Wayfinding, fire, service flow, and accessibility must not be weakened by maze-like discovery.

---

## R6｜城市自然交换 Route

### Trigger
When a larger building must bring nature, climate, and public life into architecture.

### Suitable
Collective housing, cultural buildings, campuses, urban public facilities.

### Not suitable
Pure landmark branding, insufficient maintenance resources, or climates that do not support external living.

### Core judgement
Treat balcony, roof opening, trees, shade, wind, light, and public paths as measurable systems, not naturalistic image.

### Conflict rule
Verify wind, rain, heat, ecology, structure, maintenance, privacy, and public fairness.

---

# Selection rule

- one primary Route;
- up to one secondary Route;
- explain non-applicable conditions;
- safety and operations override route purity when conflicts remain;
- do not combine all Routes.
