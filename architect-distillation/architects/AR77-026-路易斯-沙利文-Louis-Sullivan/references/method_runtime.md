# Louis Sullivan｜Method Runtime
## Evidence Audit
KNOWN — Sullivan 1896《The Tall Office Building Artistically Considered》提出高层办公建筑应从其实际条件与功能出发，并写下著名命题 “form ever follows function”。该文本同时把底层、办公重复层与顶部/设备层视为具有不同角色的问题，而不是简单历史立面套式。
KNOWN — Art Institute of Chicago 指出，后世只把 Sullivan 当结构理性主义先驱会严重删减其思想；他的 writings、drawings 和 built work 把 ornament 放在建筑实践中心。
KNOWN — Art Institute 的 Sullivan ornament 研究指出，他认为建筑的艺术/情感表达与 ornament 紧密相关；成熟 ornament 由自然启发，并从材料、结构与项目的 organic design process 中生长，而不是独立附贴。
KNOWN — NPS Wainwright 资料确认该建筑采用当时现代高层技术，包括钢框架、楼板/墙体支承与可移动隔墙，同时外部以连续竖向构件和不同层级表达高层整体。
SUPPORTED INTERPRETATION — 可迁移 Method Kernel 是“真实用途/类型 → 整体构成 → 结构与重复逻辑 → 局部表达/ornament 有机归属”，而不是“功能主义=无装饰”。
SUPPORTED INTERPRETATION — Sullivan 的 natural/organic language 可转译为 whole-to-part generative coherence；不得把植物图案本身当默认 DNA。

## Kernel
1. Ask what the building is and what activities/roles are genuinely different.
2. Give differentiated functions legible spatial roles without fragmenting the whole.
3. Establish an overall proportional/compositional order appropriate to the building type.
4. Let repetition, structure, entry, public interface and termination participate in expression when they are real project conditions.
5. Generate detail/ornament from the governing idea, material/construction logic and location in the whole.
6. Re-check integrity: no part should claim a role the program/structure does not support.

## Important Correction
“form follows function”不是删除 ornament，也不是一对一机械映射。Sullivan 本人的 ornament 是其建筑思想核心证据之一。
